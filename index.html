Secret Hygiene Check $9

Your agent was asked to fix a failing test. It read the traceback, then opened .env "to understand the config". Your Stripe and AWS keys are now in the model's context and in the chat log.

This runs before that. It walks the repository, finds likely credentials, and returns a report with file, line, and a masked fingerprint — never the value. The skill forbids the agent from opening the flagged file to double-check.

  • Nine credential patterns: AWS, GitHub, Stripe live, Slack, Google, OpenAI, JWT, private-key blocks, generic assignments
  • Name-based check for .env, .pem, .key, .git-credentials, .npmrc, .netrc, id_rsa
  • Python standard library only. No network, no writes, no dependencies
  • Tested on a real 335-file tree: zero false positives; on a planted tree: 5 of 5 caught

Free version available: finds the four patterns that cause the most damage, plus the files that leak most often.

Chinese AI-Flavor Remover $7

English humanizers do not work on Chinese. The heaviest tells are not word choice — they are 公文体黑话, stacked four-character idioms, explicit 不是 A 而是 B contrast, and bold inline labels followed by colons.

The harder part runs the other way: human Chinese writing uses more superlatives and more vague hedges than AI does, because models avoid committing. A word blacklist makes it worse.

  • Judgment rules instead of a ban list
  • Four-tier phrase table: delete outright, replace by context, Chinese-specific, hardest to spot
  • A reverse list of words you must not delete
  • Fifteen before/after rewrites

Free version: three deletion rules and one rewrite test.
